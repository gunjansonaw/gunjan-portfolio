Privacy notice

What the contact form records, and why.

Last updated 21 November 2026

The short version: you can read this site without giving me anything. If you send a message through the contact form, I also receive your IP address and approximate location. I use those only to stop spam and abuse, and I delete everything within 90 days unless I need it to deal with abuse.

What the form records

When you send a message, I receive:

  • the name, email address and message you type;
  • your IP address;
  • your approximate location: city, region and country. My hosting provider estimates this from your IP address. It isn’t GPS, I never ask your device for its location, and it’s often only roughly right;
  • the time you sent it.

You’re told this above the Send button, and you have to tick a box to confirm you’ve read it before the form will send.

Why

  • Your name, email address and message: so I can read them and reply.
  • Your IP address and approximate location: to spot and block spam, abuse and harassment. For example, the form limits how many messages one address can send, and I can block an address that misuses it.

I don’t use any of it for advertising or profiling, and I never sell it.

Who handles it

  • Vercel hosts this site and runs the form (Vercel’s privacy notice).
  • Resend delivers your message to my inbox as an email (Resend’s privacy policy).
  • I’m the only person who reads the messages.

If a message is threatening or unlawful, I may keep it longer and share it, with its record, with the relevant platform or the authorities.

How long it’s kept

Up to 90 days, then I delete it, unless I need it longer to deal with abuse. The form’s server logs, which note the IP address and country of each attempt, are kept only briefly by Vercel.

Your choices

  • Don’t want your IP address and location recorded? Email me directly. The form is just one way to reach me.
  • Want a copy of what I have, a correction, or for me to delete it sooner? Email me and I’ll take care of it.

Just browsing?

This site has no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. Like any website, its host (Vercel) processes your IP address to deliver pages and keeps standard request logs. The fonts come from Google Fonts, so Google also receives your IP address when it serves them (Google’s privacy policy).
