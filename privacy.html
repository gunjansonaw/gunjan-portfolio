Privacy notice

What this site logs, and why.

Last updated 21 November 2026

The short version: to prevent abuse, every visit to this site and every use of the contact form is logged with your IP address, approximate location and device details. I use the log only to spot and block spam, scraping and harassment, and each entry is deleted automatically after 120 days.

What’s logged when you visit

Each time you open a page, the log records:

  • the time and the page you opened;
  • your IP address;
  • your approximate location: city, region and country, which my hosting provider estimates from your IP address. It isn’t GPS, your device is never asked for its location, and it’s often only roughly right;
  • your device details: browser and version, operating system and device type (phone, tablet, computer or bot), read from the “user-agent” text your browser sends with every request. The user-agent text itself is stored too.

The notice at the top of every page says this. Because a visit is logged as soon as a page loads, there’s no way to browse the site without it.

What’s logged when you use the contact form

Each attempt to send the form logs the same details as a visit, plus the name and email address you entered and what happened (for example, sent, or blocked as spam). Your message itself isn’t kept in the log. It’s emailed to me with your IP address, approximate location and device details attached. You’re told this above the Send button, and you have to tick a box to confirm before the form will send.

Why

  • To spot and block spam, bots, scraping, harassment and other abuse. For example, the form limits how many messages one IP address can send, and I can block an address that misuses it.
  • To read and reply to messages sent through the form.

I don’t use any of it for advertising or profiling, I don’t track you across other sites, and I never sell it. There are no cookies.

Who handles it

  • Vercel hosts this site and runs the logging and the form (Vercel’s privacy notice).
  • Upstash stores the log for me, through Vercel.
  • Resend delivers contact form messages to my inbox as emails (Resend’s privacy policy).
  • I’m the only person who looks at the log and the messages.

If something is threatening or unlawful, I may keep the relevant entries and messages longer and share them with the relevant platform or the authorities.

How long it’s kept

120 days. Log entries delete themselves automatically, and I delete contact form emails by the same deadline, unless I need them longer to deal with abuse. My hosting provider’s own short-lived server logs are separate and are kept only briefly.

Your choices

  • Rather not use the form? Use the Email me link on the homepage instead.
  • Want a copy of what’s logged about you, or want it deleted sooner? Email me with roughly when you visited, and your IP address if you know it, and I’ll take care of it.

Fonts

The fonts come from Google Fonts, so Google also receives your IP address when it serves them (Google’s privacy policy).
