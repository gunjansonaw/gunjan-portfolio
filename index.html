01 · Agents

Tool use, scoped by role

A hand-built tool-use loop on the Claude API. Each agent only gets its own user’s tools, every call is audit-logged, and the app keeps working in a rules-only mode without an API key.

02 · Human in the loop

AI proposes, people approve

Tools that change data (book, cancel, invoice, message) only create an action card. Nothing is written until a person approves, and it’s re-validated at that moment.

03 · Clinical safety

Guardrails that don’t guess

Emergency symptoms are caught without calling the model. Prescriptions are checked for allergies, drug interactions, duplicate therapy and dose limits, before and after saving.

04 · Production

Shipped like a real service

Six services on Kubernetes with TLS, NetworkPolicies and non-root, read-only containers. CI runs the tests and ships multi-arch images; row locks prevent double-booking and overpayment.
